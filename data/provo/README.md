# Provo Corpus teaching summaries

These tables derive from the Provo Corpus eye-tracking release described by
Luke and Christianson (2018) and archived in the [Provo OSF
project](https://osf.io/sjefs/). The study measured reading across 55 short
English passages and supplied cloze-based predictability norms plus linguistic
and eye-movement variables. The original paper uses the corpus to establish the
resource, report its descriptive properties, and demonstrate relations among
predictability and reading-time measures.

`content-word-positions.csv` contains 1,593 complete content-word positions
from all 55 passages. A row is a word position aggregated across readers, not a
participant trial. The table retains source lexical, positional, and
predictability measurements. It filters to content words with complete source
measurements and positive first-fixation duration, then records the mean log
first-fixation duration and contributing fixation count at each position.
Reader-level dependence is consequently not recoverable from this table, while
passage and position structure remain explicit.

`passage-summaries.csv` contains one row per passage. It records position
counts, measurement means, noun and verb counts, and the within-passage noun
minus verb mean log first-fixation-duration contrast. Every retained passage
contains at least four noun and four verb positions. This second aggregation
supports a transparent passage-level inference sequence, but it does not turn
the passages into a probability sample of English texts.

Revalidate and derive both tables with:

```sh
Rscript scripts/data-grounding/prepare_provo.R
```

The earlier raw-to-position transformation is recorded in
`problem-sets/ps2/scripts/prepare_data.R`; that file and its assignment data are
read-only inputs. No participant-level rows or passage text are copied into the
notes-owned tables.

## Frozen posterior draws

`provo-stan-draw-cache.rds` contains the production NUTS draws, diagnostics,
generated quantities, and the deliberately coarse unadapted stress run used in
the Bayesian-computation notes. The notes display the complete Stan program and
R sampling calls; the frozen object keeps repeated page renders deterministic
and avoids recompiling and refitting the same model on every diagnostic page.
It is generated by
`scripts/data-grounding/reference_inference_bayesian.R --refresh-stan` from
`passage-summaries.csv` and
`scripts/data-grounding/reference_inference_bayesian.stan`. Its model,
sampling settings, seeds, dimensions, and checksum are recorded in
`posterior-cache-provenance.json`.
